I am a teaching assistant and Ph.D. candidate at Universidad
Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), supervised by Dr. José Luis Blanco
Murillo. Since September 2022, I have been a member of the GAPS
research group.

Previously, I completed my Master's in Telecommunication Engineering
at UPM. I also worked for two years as a Computer Vision Engineer at
Sigma Cognition S.L., developing computer vision solutions for
industrial applications.

I work in the field of Computer Vision, with research interests
focused on self-supervised representation learning for image and video
foundation models.

**Research keywords**
*Computer Vision, Self-Supervised Learning, Manifold Geometry*
