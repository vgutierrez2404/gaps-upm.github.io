I am a PhD student at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) and a member of the GAPS research group, under the supervision of Dr. Juan Parras and Alejandro Almodóvar. My research explores the intersection of causal deep learning and generative AI, with a particular focus on integrating causal inference, deep generative models, and large language models. I have a background in Biomedical Engineering and a Master's degree with a specialization in Machine Learning. Alongside my academic research, I worked on generative AI at Roche, with a focus on natural language processing, AI evaluation, and agentic AI systems. I am currently an Agentic AI Engineer at Roche Switzerland, where I develop AI solutions for clinical trial systems.
