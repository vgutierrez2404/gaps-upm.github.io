Mar Elizo is a Ph.D. candidate in the Communications Technologies and Systems program at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), where she is a member of the Signal Processing Applications Group (GAPS). She is supervised by Dr. Juan Parras Moral. She holds a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M) and an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence, Pattern Recognition and Digital Imaging from Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV). Her research focuses on the application of artificial intelligence to healthcare and personalized medicine, with an emphasis on synthetic patient generation and deep generative models, particularly interested in preserving clinically meaningful causal relationships and the validation of synthetic data quality.

Prior to joining UPM, she completed internships at the Health Predictive Analytics Department of the Instituto de Ingeniería del Conocimiento (IIC) and collaborated with the Valencian Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence (VRAIN), where she worked on the application of artificial intelligence techniques to emotion analysis.

**Research Keywords**

*Deep Generative Models, Causal Machine Learning, Synthetic Data Generation*
