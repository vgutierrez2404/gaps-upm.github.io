Juan Parras is an Associate Professor (Profesor Contratado Doctor) at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), where he has worked since 2015. He holds a PhD in Telecommunication Technologies and Systems (UPM, 2020, Cum Laude, International Mention), a Master's in Telecommunications Engineering (UPM, 2016, GPA 9.11, awarded for Excellent Academic Performance), and a Bachelor's in Telecommunication Technologies Engineering (Universidad de Jaén, 2014, GPA 9.59, Best Academic Record).

His research sits at the intersection of optimization (including optimal control and game theory) and statistical learning, with applications in AI for healthcare, communications, autonomous underwater navigation, and industry. His research has been funded by several funded projects, including European ones such as GENOMED4 ALL, SYNTHEMA, REPO4EU and SYNTHIA. His research results have been published in internationally recognized venues: to date, Juan has authored more than 50 publications, with more than 30 high-impact journals. Being committed to facilitating the replication and dissemination of his research results, his webpage contains the code for most of his research outputs.

Juan also plays a significant role in education, including teaching at UPM since 2019 and mentoring numerous young researchers actively, including PhD students, as well as Bachelor and Master students.

Finally, Juan has been recognized with several distinctions, including the Best Signal Processing Thesis from the Spanish Chapter of IEEE in Communications and Signal Processing.
