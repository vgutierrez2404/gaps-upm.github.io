Patricia A. Apellániz has been an Assistant Professor at the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid since June 2026, where she teaches courses in audio processing and machine learning. She is also a researcher at the Signal Processing Applications Group (GAPS) at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM).

She received her PhD in Communications Technologies and Systems from UPM in 2025, with a thesis focused on deep generative models for survival analysis and synthetic data generation in healthcare.

Her research focuses on the development of machine learning and deep generative models for healthcare, with particular emphasis on survival analysis, synthetic data generation, federated learning, and explainable AI. Her work addresses key challenges in medical AI, including data scarcity, data censoring, privacy constraints, and the limited availability of representative clinical datasets. In this context, she has developed generative and federated learning approaches to improve prognostic modeling, generate high-quality synthetic tabular healthcare data, and enable privacy-preserving collaboration across clinical institutions.

She also participates in European medical research projects, contributing to the development of AI-based tools for clinical data analysis, synthetic data generation, and medical decision support. These projects are closely aligned with her research interests and aim to translate advanced machine learning methods into practical solutions for healthcare applications. In addition, she collaborates with CSIC on applying machine learning and synthetic data generation techniques to solid-state battery diagnostics, exploring how AI-based models can support the analysis and interpretation of electrochemical data in contexts where experimental data are scarce or costly to obtain.

**Research keywords**

*Deep Generative Models, Interpretable AI, Synthetic Data Generation, Federated Learning, Survival Analysis*
