Alba Garrido López is a teaching assistant and Ph.D. candidate in the Communications Technologies and Systems program at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM), supervised by Dr. Juan Parras Moral within the Signal Processing Applications Group (GAPS). She holds a B.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering and an M.Sc. in Signal Theory and Communications, with a specialization in Machine Learning for Big Data, both from UPM.

Prior to her doctoral studies, she worked at Hospital Universitario La Paz, applying deep learning techniques to neuroimaging data segmentation. Currently, her research focuses on the application of artificial intelligence to personalized medicine, with a particular emphasis on deep generative models, survival analysis, and synthetic data generation for multimodal biomedical data processing and disease diagnosis.

**Research Keywords**
*Deep Generative Models,  Survival Analysis, Synthetic Data Generation*
